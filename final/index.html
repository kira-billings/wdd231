The Recipe Box That Started It All

Grandma Aina Harrison (1917–2011) was known in our family as a wonderful cook. She shared that talent generously—even once making thirty individual apple pies for a grandson to take to his kindergarten class. Her handwritten recipes and well-loved recipe box inspired this collection.
