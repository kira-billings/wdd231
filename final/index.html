From Our Recipe Box to Yours

The Recipe Box That Started It All

Grandma Aina Harrison (1917–2011) was known in our family as a wonderful cook. She shared that talent generously—even once making thirty individual apple pies for a grandson to take to his kindergarten class. Her handwritten recipes and well-loved recipe box inspired this collection.

Preserving Family History

Sometimes the best things come to us at just the right time. As I was preparing to create this website for a school project, I inherited my Grandma Aina Harrison’s old recipe box, filled with handwritten recipes collected over many years. Her colorful, old-fashioned recipe box became the inspiration for this site—and what began as a school assignment became a way to preserve a little piece of our family history.

Preserving the Love

These recipes come from many generations, including Grandma Aina, Grandma Fizz, Grandma Una, Grandma Thelma, Grandma Linda, Grandpa Bob, Grandma Judy, Grandma Kira (that’s me—I’m a grandma now too!), and others whose names may have been lost along the way. Whether you’re making an old favorite or discovering a recipe for the first time, I hope this collection brings back memories of family, home, and the love that has always found its way into our food.
